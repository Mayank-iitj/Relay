# Product: Relay

## Platform
web

## Stack
Next.js, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion. Chosen for precision, performance, and a high-density "industrial" feel.

## Users
Engineering Leads, Core Maintainers, Successors, and Engineering Managers/Compliance Officers.

## Product Purpose
Relay is an enterprise knowledge handover platform. It extracts and reconciles project state from scattered communication trails (Slack, Git, Jira) into an immutable, role-governed Transition Dossier. It prevents "institutional amnesia" during engineer offboarding.

## Positioning
"The Immutable Record of Truth." Relay is an operational data infrastructure tool, not a conversational AI. It is the "GitHub for Handovers."

## Operating Context
High-stakes corporate engineering transitions. The environment is one of stress, information overload, and strict compliance/security requirements.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Multi-source ingestion (Git, Slack, Jira).
- PII Redaction at ingest.
- Pairwise contradiction detection based on temporal assertions.
- Role-based projections (ABAC) to prevent permission leakage.
- Cryptographic sign-off for immutable records.

## Brand Commitments
- **Name:** Relay
- **Voice:** Deterministic, authoritative, transparent, and utilitarian.
- **Identity:** Industrial Data Tool. High information density, tabular, muted, and precise.

## Product Principles
1. **Utility-First, Zero Chatbotism:** No conversational prompts or "sparkles." Pure data infrastructure.
2. **Strict Verifiability:** Every claim must have a citation chip to an immutable source.
3. **Explicit Uncertainty:** Contradictions are surfaced for human arbitration, not synthesized.
4. **Permission-Bounded:** Data is partitioned at the node level; role projections are server-side.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Standard WCAG 2.1 AA. Priority on scanability and high-contrast typography for technical data.
